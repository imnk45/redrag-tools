How these times are calculated

Download time = (game size in MB × 8) ÷ effective speed in Mbps. Game sizes use binary units (1 GB = 1,024 MB), the way Windows and game launchers display them. Effective speed is 90% of your plan speed, because no home connection holds 100% of its advertised rate for hours. Most charts on the internet quietly assume 100%, which is why their times always look a little too good. Ours are the honest version: if a game says 5h 33m here, that is roughly what you will actually wait.

Game sizes are from the REDRAG Tools game database (last updated October 2026). Sizes grow slowly as games receive patches and DLC, so treat every figure as a close estimate, not a promise.

The games everyone asks about

How long does GTA V take to download?

Grand Theft Auto V is about 110 GB with GTA Online updates included. On a typical 50 Mbps connection that is 5h 33m; on 100 Mbps it drops to 2h 46m. On a slow 10 Mbps line it takes 1d 3h 48m, so start it before bed and it will still be going the next evening.

How long does Call of Duty take to download?

Call of Duty: Black Ops 6 is about 150 GB and Warzone about 100 GB. Black Ops 6 takes 7h 35m at 50 Mbps and 3h 47m at 100 Mbps. Warzone takes 5h 3m at 50 Mbps. Call of Duty HQ shares files between titles, so owning one game can shrink the other’s download.

How long does Fortnite take to download?

Fortnite is about 35 GB: 1h 46m at 50 Mbps, 53m at 100 Mbps, and just 5m on gigabit fibre. Seasonal updates are far smaller than the base install, usually a few GB.

How long does Valorant take to download?

Valorant is about 30 GB through the Riot Client: 1h 31m at 50 Mbps and 45m at 100 Mbps. The small installer you download first is only the launcher; the real download starts inside the Riot Client.

What is the biggest game download on this chart?

God of War Ragnarök, at about 190 GB. At 50 Mbps that is 9h 36m, and at 25 Mbps it is 19h 12m. The smallest full game here is Valheim at 3 GB: just 9m at 50 Mbps.

Why your real download may be slower than this chart

This chart assumes a steady wired connection at 90% efficiency. The same 110 GB GTA V download on a 100 Mbps plan changes a lot with your setup:

ConnectionGTA V (110 GB) at 100 Mbps
Advertised speed, perfect conditions (100%)2h 30m

Wi-Fi through walls (75%)3h 20m

Three more honest warnings: game launchers rarely use your full bandwidth at peak launch hours; a nearly-full SSD can slow the install phase after the download; and on mobile hotspots, speeds swing minute to minute. If your time matters, download overnight and on a wired connection where you can.

Quick answers

How long does a 100 GB game take to download?
At 50 Mbps, about 5h 3m. At 100 Mbps, about 2h 31m. At 25 Mbps, about 10h 6m. These are real-world times at 90% efficiency.

How long does 1 GB take to download?
About 3 minutes at 50 Mbps, about a minute and a half at 100 Mbps, and 15 minutes at 10 Mbps. Small files feel instant on modern plans; big games are where the hours go.

Why is my download slower than my speed test?
Speed tests measure a short burst to a nearby server. Game downloads are hours-long transfers from busier servers, and Wi-Fi, other devices and ISP congestion all eat into the rate. This is exactly why this chart uses 90% efficiency instead of the headline speed.

Does download size equal install size?
Not always. The download is compressed; the installed game is usually a little larger once unpacked, and it keeps growing with patches. Plan free space above the download size, not equal to it.
