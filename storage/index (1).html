How to Use This Game Storage Calculator

Planning your game library storage doesn't have to be guesswork. Our Game Storage Planner helps you calculate exactly how many games fit on your SSD before you buy or upgrade. Whether you're building a new gaming PC, buying a PS5, or expanding your Xbox Series X storage, this tool gives you precise numbers.

Why Use a Game Storage Calculator?

Modern games are massive. Call of Duty: Black Ops 6 alone takes 235GB — that's nearly 25% of a 1TB SSD's usable space. GTA VI is expected to require 150GB. Without proper planning, you might buy a 1TB SSD thinking it's enough, only to find you can install just 6-7 AAA games.

Our calculator accounts for:

  • Real game install sizes — Updated for 2026 with 100+ popular titles
  • Platform-specific OS overhead — PS5 reserves ~158GB, Xbox ~198GB, PC ~93GB from a 1TB drive
  • Usable vs advertised space — A "1TB" SSD only has 931GB formatted on PC
  • Future updates — Games grow over time with patches and DLC

Is 512GB SSD Enough for Gaming in 2026?

No — 512GB is no longer enough for most gamers. Here's the reality:

  • A 512GB SSD has only 390-465GB of usable space depending on platform
  • Two modern AAA games (COD: 235GB + GTA VI: 150GB) = 385GB — your entire drive
  • PS5's 512GB model gives only ~390GB usable after OS reservation
  • You'll constantly uninstall and reinstall games, which wears the SSD and wastes time

Who is 512GB for? Only casual gamers who play 1-2 competitive games (Valorant, CS2, Fortnite) and nothing else. Even then, future updates will push you to the limit within a year.

Our recommendation: Skip 512GB entirely. The price difference to 1TB is usually only ₹1,500-2,500, and the convenience is worth 10x that amount.
